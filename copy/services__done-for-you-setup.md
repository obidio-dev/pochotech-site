## How the session works

**Before the call.** You buy the template, add the setup option, and book a time on my calendar. I send a short list of the logins and access the workflow needs, so the session starts at full speed.

**On the call — 60 to 90 minutes.** I connect each credential, configure the workflow for your accounts, and run it live until it behaves on your real data. Then I walk you through how it works and where to adjust it.

**After the call.** The workflow is live in your accounts, and the recording arrives so anyone on your team can replay the entire setup.

## What you walk away with

- The workflow running in your accounts, under your credentials
- A recording of the full session
- The template's step-by-step PDF guide and 90-second walkthrough video
- Working knowledge of where to change it when your process changes

## Questions before you book a session

### What I need from you before the call

Admin access to the tools the template connects — for the Business Card Scanner, that's Slack, OpenAI, and HubSpot. The exact list for your template is in its PDF guide, and I send it again when you book.

### Whether you could set it up yourself instead

Yes — every template ships with the workflow JSON, a step-by-step PDF, and a walkthrough video, and most go live in under an hour on your own. The session is for teams that want it running today, verified live on their data, with a recording to keep.

### What happens if the template doesn't match your stack

Each template works as described with the tools listed on its page.
