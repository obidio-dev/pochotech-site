# Who I am and how I work

I'm Erick Bonilla — founder and the only engineer at pocho.tech. Every workflow on this site I scoped and built myself, and most of them I still operate.

pocho.tech is the AI automation studio of Erick Bonilla. He designs and builds custom AI-powered automations for small teams — event agencies, media companies, tourism organizations, and accounting firms — working embedded inside each client's tools and team.

That's the formal version. The practical version: you tell me where your team's hours go, and I build the system that gets them back.

## What embedded looks like, week to week

Most engagements don't end at handoff — the three client teams on this site all kept me on after the first build. Here is what an ordinary week looks like.

**I'm in your Slack.** Requests, fixes, and ideas happen in the channels where your team already works.

**I work inside your accounts.** Workflows run on your systems under your credentials; when I build or fix something, it happens where you can see it.

**We have a standing touchpoint.** A weekly check-in covers what shipped, what broke, and what's worth building next.

That rhythm is how one automation at Dahlia+ became four, and one system at TPS Workforce Hub became six.

## The three teams

- **Dahlia+ Agency** (event production, team of six to eight) — four automations, including the proposal-to-invoice pipeline that cut processing from 1.5–2 hours to 15 minutes.
- **Matador Network** (travel media, editorial team of twelve) — four automations, including a newsletter curator that reads 400+ articles a day and a creator tracker covering 70 creators.
- **TPS Workforce Hub** (clean-tech workforce training, Boston) — six connected systems running admissions and operations, from AI-scored interviews to the 5pm report the whole team reads.

## What I build with

I build on n8n and Make.com, with the OpenAI and Anthropic APIs handling the reasoning. Across twelve automations in production, that stack connects more than twenty tools — HubSpot, Airtable, Monday.com, QuickBooks, Slack, Supabase, Apify, Google Analytics 4, and others.

I also speak about this work at industry conferences. Matador Network hired me to build after I taught n8n to their AI Advocacy Director — teaching the tools and building with them are the same job to me.
