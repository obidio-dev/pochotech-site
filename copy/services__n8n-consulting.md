## Questions teams ask before a consulting engagement

### n8n vs Zapier vs Make — which to use

Zapier is the fastest start for simple triggers; Make.com handles longer visual scenarios at moderate cost; n8n gives the most control — self-hostable, with code where you need it, and well suited to AI-heavy workflows. I build client systems on both n8n and Make.com, so the recommendation isn't a pitch for either platform.

The honest answer depends on your volume, your data constraints, and who maintains it after I leave. It's the first ten minutes of the call.

### Whether I review existing workflows or only build new ones

Both. Architecture review of what you already have is a common starting point — most teams arrive with something half-working rather than a blank slate.

### Self-hosted n8n versus n8n cloud

Cloud is the right default while you're proving that workflows matter to the business. Self-hosting earns its overhead when volume, cost, or data control demand it — and if you go that way, the setup is part of what I cover.
