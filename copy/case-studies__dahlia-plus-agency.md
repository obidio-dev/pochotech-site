# Dahlia+ Agency

**Event production and consulting — [dahliaplus.com](https://dahliaplus.com).** Team of six to eight, led by Dahlia El Gazzar. Four automations in production, on an embedded retainer.

---

## The before

An accepted proposal set off the same manual relay every time: update the deal in HubSpot, build the project board in Monday.com, create the customer in QuickBooks, draft two invoices, split 50/50. Done by hand, it took an hour and a half to two hours.

At two to four proposals a day, that relay was most of someone's day. Six to eight hours of manual operations, every working day, inside a team of six to eight people.

Leads had the same shape at smaller scale. Web form submissions, referrals arriving by email, business cards collected at events — each one re-typed into HubSpot, or lost on the way there.

---

## What I built

Four automations run the Dahlia+ back office.

1. **Proposal-to-invoice pipeline** — An accepted proposal in Fresh Proposals updates the HubSpot deal stage, builds the Monday.com board, and creates the QuickBooks customer with two invoices split 50/50.
2. **Web form lead routing** — Every form submission creates the HubSpot contact, adds the subscriber in Beehiiv, and posts to Slack so the team sees the lead the moment it arrives.
3. **AI referral capture** — An AI parses referral emails as they land and creates the contact in HubSpot, so an introduction doesn't depend on someone remembering to copy it over.
4. **Business card scanner** — Drop a photo in Slack; GPT-4o Vision reads the card, extracts every field, and creates the HubSpot contact.

---

**1.5–2 hours → 15 minutes**

**2–4 a day**
