## Questions I get on every discovery call

**What a custom automation costs**

Builds start at $1,500, with a fixed price set in writing after the discovery call — what it costs depends on how many systems are involved and how much of the logic needs AI. If you start with the $500 audit, that full amount is credited toward the build.

**How long a build takes**

One to four weeks for most projects, depending on how many systems are involved and how much testing the workflow needs against your real data. The timeline is written into the scope document before any work begins, so you know the delivery date before you commit.

**Who maintains it after handoff**

You get documentation and a recorded walkthrough at handoff, so your team can run everything on its own. In practice, most clients keep me on — all three teams on this site continue on embedded retainers, and when something breaks, you message the person who wrote it.

**Who owns the workflows**

You do — everything runs in your accounts under your credentials, on your n8n or Make.com instance, with your API keys. If we ever part ways, every workflow keeps running exactly as it did the day before; nothing I build depends on infrastructure that belongs to me.

**Why n8n and Make.com instead of Zapier**

Zapier is fine for simple triggers, but the systems I build — AI evaluation pipelines, multi-step branching logic, two-way syncs — need real conditionals, sub-workflows, and direct API calls. n8n and Make.com handle that at a fraction of the per-task cost, and n8n can run on your own server.

**What the $500 audit includes**

A 60-minute review of how your team actually works, then a written report within five business days: what to automate first, which tools to use, estimated time savings, and the build scope for each recommendation. Commission a build afterward and the full $500 comes off the price.

**Whether templates require technical skill**

Each template includes the n8n workflow JSON, a step-by-step PDF setup guide, and a walkthrough video — if you can follow instructions and paste API credentials, you can install one. If you'd rather not, Done-For-You Setup adds $100 and I configure it with you on a live call.

**Whether I take ongoing retainer work**

Yes — embedded retainers are how most of my work runs. All three client teams on this site started with a single build and kept me on afterward, working inside their Slack and their tools; each retainer is scoped per engagement on the discovery call.
