The Sheets ↔ HubSpot Task Manager is a free n8n workflow: a live two-way sync between a Google Sheet and HubSpot tasks — create, update, and complete in either, and both stay current.

## What it does

- Add a task row in the Google Sheet and the task appears in HubSpot
- Create or change a task in HubSpot and the Sheet updates to match
- Complete a task on either side and both reflect it
- The sync runs continuously in your own n8n instance
