## What a custom build looks like

The clearest example runs at Dahlia+ Agency, an event production company with a team of six to eight.

When a proposal is accepted in Fresh Proposals, the automation updates the HubSpot deal stage, builds the Monday.com board, and creates the QuickBooks customer with two invoices split 50/50 — untouched by anyone. Processing one proposal went from 1.5–2 hours to 15 minutes.

At 2–4 proposals a day, that's 6–8 hours of manual operations gone from every working day.

Other builds running in production:

- **AI interview evaluation — TPS Workforce Hub.** Read.ai interview transcripts arrive by webhook; candidates get matched and objectively scored, and human reviewers get a structured brief with direct quotes from the interview.
- **AI newsletter curator — Matador Network.** Overnight, an AI reads 400+ articles across eleven-plus publications, picks the top ten, and delivers a finished newsletter every morning with zero human input.
- **Business card scanner — Dahlia+ Agency.** Drop a photo in Slack; GPT-4o Vision reads the card, extracts every field, and creates the HubSpot contact.
