### Social media creator tracker

The tracker scrapes TikTok and Instagram for each creator's posts and delivers a Monday digest — every URL with views and likes attached. Tracking went from 5 hours a week to 1.5, while coverage grew from 30 creators to 70.

Once a month, the system searches Eventbrite, Meetup, and Luma across fifteen US cities and delivers the results to the team. Nobody opens fifteen browser tabs.
