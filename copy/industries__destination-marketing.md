## 2. Where the hours go

A DMO's week is full of work that scales with partner count: tracking what member businesses are posting, opening, and hosting. Most of it lives in spreadsheets that are current only on the day someone updates them.

Planner inquiries and visitor leads arrive by form and email, and qualifying them means reading each one. Partner outreach is the same email written over and over, personalized by hand when there's time.

## 3. What I build for DMOs

### Partner tracking

An automation that watches your partners' channels — social posts, event listings, announcements — and delivers a digest with every URL and its numbers attached. The same mechanism runs in production at Matador Network, where it tracks 70 creators across TikTok and Instagram in 1.5 hours of staff time a week.

**Tools:** Apify, OpenAI, Google Sheets or your CRM

### AI email agents

An AI that drafts partner and planner emails from your CRM history — it reads each relationship's past correspondence and writes the check-in or follow-up in your voice, with a review step if you want one. A generalized version is in the template catalog: AI Relationship Nurturing Engine, $299 → https://checkout.lemonsqueezy.com/buy/1320599

**Tools:** HubSpot or any CRM, OpenAI, Gmail

### Content pipelines

An AI that reads your region's sources overnight — local news, partner feeds, event calendars — and delivers a curated newsletter every morning. The production version of this system processes 400+ articles a day at Matador Network.

**Tools:** Apify, OpenAI, Gmail

### Lead qualification

An automation that takes each inbound lead — planner RFPs, group inquiries, web forms — scores it against your criteria, and creates the CRM record with the score and reasoning attached. I run AI scoring of this kind in production at TPS Workforce Hub, where it turns interview transcripts into structured briefs for human reviewers.

**Tools:** your CRM, OpenAI
