## What the report contains

Four things, specific to your team and your tools:

- **What to automate first** — your workflows ranked by hours recovered against effort to build
- **The tools for each** — which platforms and APIs every recommendation would connect
- **Estimated time savings** — what each automation should give back, per week
- **A build scope per recommendation** — what it would cost and roughly how long it would take

Whether you build with me, build in-house, or shelve it for a quarter, you have the plan in writing.

## How it works

**The call — 60 minutes.** You walk me through how the work actually happens, ideally with the person who does it day to day on the call. [Book it directly on my calendar](https://calendly.com/erick-pocho/1hr-session).

**The report — within five business days.** A written document covering the four sections above, specific to your tools and your volume.

## Why start here

A custom build is $1,500 or more, and the right first automation isn't always obvious from inside the team. The audit answers that in writing before you commit to anything.

If you commission a build afterward, the full $500 comes off the price. If you don't, you keep a plan any competent engineer could execute.

Sometimes the report says a $249 template covers it, or that a workflow isn't worth automating yet. You get that in writing too.
