# Matador Network

**Travel media — [matadornetwork.com](https://matadornetwork.com).** Around 50 people, with an editorial team of twelve. Matador also operates GuideGeek, an AI travel product for destination marketing organizations. Four automations in production.

---

## The before

Creator tracking meant opening TikTok and Instagram, finding every post, and copying URLs, views, and likes into a sheet by hand. Five hours a week covered 30 creators, and the program needed to be larger.

The morning news sweep had the same shape. Eleven-plus industry publications produce roughly 400 articles a day; an editorial team of twelve needed the ten worth reading.

Affiliate reporting and event research rounded it out — more data someone had to go collect before the editorial work could start.

---

## What I built

Four automations run the editorial back office.

1. **Creator tracker** — Scrapes TikTok and Instagram for every tracked creator and delivers a Monday digest of post URLs, views, and likes.
2. **AI newsletter curator** — Apify collects roughly 400 articles a day from eleven-plus publications overnight; GPT-4o picks the top ten, writes the summaries, and a polished HTML newsletter arrives every morning.
3. **Affiliate link performance report** — Every Monday, GA4 data becomes a report ranking the top 20 affiliate links, with AI insights.
4. **Event search** — A monthly sweep of Eventbrite, Meetup, and Luma across 15 US cities.

---

## The numbers

**5 hrs/week → 1.5 hrs**
Creator tracking, by hand versus automated.

**30 → 70**
Creators tracked, same workflow.
