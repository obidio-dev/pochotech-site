Three problems. One system that closes them.
47 years of doing great work — without a system tracking it. That's the gap. Here's what it looks like before and after.
Challenge 1 — Two showrooms, zero shared job board
Without a system
📞 Katy calls Houston to ask which installer is free next week — nobody has the answer in front of them.
📅 Material allocation lives on a whiteboard in one location. The other showroom doesn't know what's reserved.
📊 If Daniel is at the Houston shop, he can't see what Katy has in flight without calling.
⚠ Same slab gets assigned to two different jobs. One team shows up to fabricate and the material isn't there.
With your system
✓ Every active job from both showrooms on one board — stage, material, installer, install date.
✓ Material reserved on the job record — both teams see allocation before fabrication starts.
✓ Installer schedule visible across locations — no double-booking, no phone tag.
✓ Conflict alerts fire automatically when the same slab or the same installer is assigned twice.
Challenge 2 — $20K–$115K quotes with no follow-up trail
Without a system
📞 Client fills the form Friday at 5 PM. Nobody follows up until Monday. They've already called two competitors.
📋 Designer memory is the CRM. When the designer is out, the lead is invisible.
📅 No record of when a quote was sent, whether it was opened, or whether the client has gone cold.
⚠ A $72,000 kitchen remodel falls through because the follow-up came two days too late.
With your system
✓ Every inquiry captured with timestamp, project type, and estimated value — no lead falls through.
✓ Automatic confirmation sent the same day the form is submitted — client knows you're on it.
✓ Designer dashboard shows which leads are hot (under 24h), warm (24–72h), and stale (>72h). Priority is clear.
✓ Quote pipeline shows total open value — so you can see revenue that's one follow-up call away from closing.
Challenge 3 — "How's my kitchen coming along?" calls that don't need to happen
Without a system
📞 Client calls. Staff pauses. Finds the paper or calls the shop. Calls back. Every remodel project, multiple times.
🔒 Homeowner and contractor have no visibility into fabrication timeline — they're guessing.
⏳ Your best people spend part of their day answering status calls instead of fabricating or installing.
⚠ When a delay happens (material, scheduling), client finds out by calling — not because you told them.
With your system
✓ Client portal shows current stage, install date, and the last note from the designer — no phone call needed.
✓ When a date changes, the client gets notified automatically. They're not surprised; they're informed.
✓ Contractors get the same portal — they coordinate their own crews around your install schedule.
✓ Your team fabricates. Your system handles the "where are we?" conversations.
The real cost of no system isn't the chaos — it's what doesn't get built.
Bayou Stone has been the system for 47 years. That's how the business grew to 60 people and two locations — because Daniel's judgment and memory filled every gap. The problem with that model isn't quality. It's scale. Every dollar of growth from here depends on someone else being able to see what Daniel sees — which jobs are moving, which leads are hot, which materials are allocated, and which client needs a call before they call you.

A system doesn't replace that judgment. It makes it visible to the rest of the team.
