The real cost of manual operations

40 years serving Houston businesses — that volume of multi-stage projects (design, permit, fabrication, install) deserves something better than email and spreadsheets to make sure nothing falls through.

Channel letters, vehicle wraps, monument signs, fleet rebranding — each one has specs that live somewhere. Here's what the difference looks like.

Without a system

✖Quote for channel letters built from memory or Excel — recalculated every time a client requests a change
✖Project status lives in text threads — "Hey, where are my signs?" requires a manual check
✖TSCL permit deadlines tracked on a calendar note — no automatic alert when a permit is stalling
✖Corporate and fleet clients have no single place to see their order history or reorder easily
✖Upsell opportunities (LED upgrade, fleet expansion, wayfinding add-on) missed because there's no trigger

With your own system

✓Smart quote builder: pick sign type, dimensions, material, permit requirement — draft ready in 60 seconds, Michelle approves with one click
✓Four-stage pipeline (Design → Permit → Fabrication → Install) visible at a glance — clients get automatic status updates
✓Permit expiry alerts fire automatically — no permit dies silently because it fell through the cracks
✓Every corporate and fleet client has a full order history — reorders and upsells surface without outbound calls
✓Pipeline visibility by stage, client type, and revenue — Michelle sees what's closing this month without counting tabs
