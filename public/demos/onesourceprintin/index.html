03 — Institutional Client Reporting
✗ Today

A procurement officer from a government account needs the complete order history from the past 12 months for their annual audit. Grace spends half a day digging through email threads, spreadsheets, and memory.

⌛ 4–6 hours to compile. Error-prone.
→
✓ With the portal

Every order is logged automatically — product, quantity, proof version, approval date, delivery date. The client can export their own history in 30 seconds. Grace never touches it.

● One click. Auditable. No dependency on anyone's memory.
04 — Four Service Lines, One Conversation
✗ Today

A construction company needs folders (digital printing), site signage (large format), and engraved name plates (laser engraving) for a project kickoff. That's three separate conversations with three separate coordinators — and the client has to chase each one.

3 service lines = 3 disconnected threads.
→
✓ With the portal

One order. Multiple line items — digital printing, large format, laser engraving. Each routed internally to the right team. Client sees one status page. One delivery date. One conversation.

▲ Higher order value, lower coordination cost.
